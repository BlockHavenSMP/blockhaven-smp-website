Community guidelines

The rules that help BlockHaven stay welcoming and enjoyable for everyone.

Respect other players

Be kind, avoid harassment, and respect the time and effort behind other players' builds. Disagreements should be resolved calmly or brought to the moderation team.

Protect builds and resources

Do not grief, steal, exploit bugs, or bypass protected areas. Report accidental damage and suspected exploits rather than taking advantage of them.

Keep competition fair

Follow posted event rules, avoid interfering with other entries, and credit collaborators. Staff may clarify rules as events develop.

Security and account safety

Never share your Minecraft, Microsoft, Discord, or email passwords. Staff will not ask for passwords, authentication codes, or remote access. Verify unusual requests through established community channels.

Report issues responsibly

Send moderation concerns to the moderator team and technical bug reports to the appropriate staff contact. Include clear reproduction steps when possible, but never publish private account information.
