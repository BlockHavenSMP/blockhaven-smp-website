FEATURED EVENT · SEASON 4 FINALE

The Great Overworld Build-Off

Theme: Forgotten Civilizations

Tell the story of a place lost to time: an overgrown temple, a ruined capital, a desert settlement, or a world of your own design.

Oct 31Entries close
Nov 2Winners announced
3Planned judges

Competition rules

  1. Build in the designated community event area.
  2. Follow normal server rules and respect other players' builds.
  3. Submit one finished build per participant or team before October 31.
  4. Include a short description of the civilization and its story.
  5. Judging considers creativity, theme interpretation, detail, and presentation.

Judging update

Our original guest judge, EnderArtist, is no longer available because of a scheduling conflict. Casey is working with the staff team to finalize a replacement; Alex will confirm the updated judging lineup before entries close.

Event coordinator

Casey Brooks (CraftingCasey) handles event logistics and submission questions. Sam Rivera assists with staff scheduling.
