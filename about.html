OUR WORLD · OUR PEOPLE

About BlockHaven

Small enough to know your neighbors. Big enough to build something extraordinary.

A survival server with a community-first mindset

BlockHaven began in 2022 as a small group of friends looking for a long-running Java survival world. Today our whitelisted community includes around 180 members, with 15–30 players online during busy events.

Players run shops, cooperate on public builds, trade resources, and plan projects together. Our volunteer staff helps keep the world welcoming and the technical side stable.

How we play

We keep survival at the center: gathering resources, building farms, exploring, and creating lasting landmarks. Community shopping districts and seasonal events bring people together without taking away the freedom to play your own way.

Our seasons

Season 4 is winding down with the Great Overworld Build-Off. Season 5, The Ender Expansion, is targeted for November 7, 2026. The staff team is preparing a refreshed economy, updated community spaces, and infrastructure improvements.
